What this evidence means

• Current relationships are based on Sentinel's bounded TCP evidence; connections can appear or disappear immediately after collection.
• History is created only by explicit Capture History Snapshot actions and uses bounded retention.
• Cross-snapshot identity ignores transient PID changes and ESTABLISHED local ephemeral-port churn; those values remain sample context, not stable identity.
• Snapshot absence does not reveal the exact time a connection ended or prove a process never connected between snapshots.
• Sentinel does not inspect packet contents, decrypt traffic, or claim a connection is malicious from these relationships.
